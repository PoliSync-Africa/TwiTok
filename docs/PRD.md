## Youth protection

For users under 18, the platform must support jurisdiction-aware youth policies. The product baseline is:

- Maximum continuous active use: 60 minutes
- Mandatory break after the continuous-use limit: 2 hours
- Server-side enforcement
- Warnings before lock
- Parent/guardian controls
- Secure parent override
- No bypass through logout, restart, reinstall or local storage clearing

Exact age thresholds and legal requirements remain configurable by jurisdiction.
